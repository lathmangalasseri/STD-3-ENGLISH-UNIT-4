English - Standard 3

Unit 4: Blooms and Breeze Max Marks: 20
I. Read the passage and answer the questions. (4 Marks)
Bella is a beautiful blue butterfly. She lives in a colourful garden. One morning, a gentle breeze started blowing. The flowers danced in the breeze. "Come and play with us, Bella!" called the red rose. Bella fluttered her wings and flew to the rose. She drank sweet nectar.
1. Who is Bella?
2. Where does Bella live?
3. Who called Bella to play?
4. Pick out an action word (verb) from the passage.
II. Write True or False. (3 Marks)
5. The flowers cried in the breeze. [ _____ ]
6. Bella drank sweet nectar. [ _____ ]
7. Bella is a green butterfly. [ _____ ]
III. Match the following. (3 Marks)
A. Gentle1. Rose
B. Sweet2. Breeze
C. Red3. Nectar
IV. Complete the sequence of events. (5 Marks)
  1. Bella lived in a colourful garden.
  2. A gentle breeze started blowing.
  4. Suddenly, the wind blew hard.
V. Creative Writing. (5 Marks)
10. The wind blew hard and Bella had to hide under a leaf. Write Bella's diary entry for that day.
Answer Key:
1. A beautiful blue butterfly.
2. In a colourful garden.
3. The red rose.
4. lives / started / blowing / danced / called / fluttered / flew / drank.
5. False.
6. True.
7. False.
Match: A-2, B-3, C-1.
Sequence missing parts (Examples):
- The red rose called Bella to play / Bella drank sweet nectar.
- Bella hid under a big green leaf.
Diary: Should express joy of playing with flowers and the sudden fear/hiding when the hard wind blew, ending with hope or relief when sun came out.
